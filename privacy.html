Privacy policy

Hermes is a private assistant with a single user, its owner. It only accesses the Google account that the owner authorizes, and only to read calendar events for the owner's own daily summary.

  • Data stays on the owner's own server. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
